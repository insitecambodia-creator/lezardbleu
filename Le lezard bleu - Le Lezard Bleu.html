The Atelier

At Le Lézard Bleu, every piece that comes through our doors (a family photograph, a fragile watercolor, a painting passed down through generations) is treated with the same care we’d want for our own. Located in the heart of Phnom Penh, our atelier combines traditional framing craftsmanship with the technical precision of art conservation and restoration.

Whether you’re framing a new piece or bringing an old one back to life, our work is guided by one principle: respect for the material, and respect for what it means to you.

What the lezard does

Custom Framing

From family photos to fine art, we design frames that protect your pieces and complement them, tailored to the work, not off the shelf. Every material and finish is chosen with the piece in mind.

Art Conservation

Artwork ages, and not always gracefully. We use conservation-grade materials and techniques to protect pieces from light, humidity, and time, so what you love today still looks its best tomorrow.

Restoration

Torn, faded, water-damaged, or simply worn by age, we bring damaged pieces back to life with careful, hands-on restoration work, treating each piece as if it were irreplaceable. Because often, it is.

Fine Art Printing

A good print deserves the same care as the frame around it. In our atelier, we print photographs, drawings, and artwork on a professional large-format printer using archival pigment inks and quality art papers, so the detail, depth, and tone of the original come through.Whether you’re reproducing an artist’s work, enlarging a treasured photograph, or preparing a piece for exhibition, we can print it and frame it, all under one roof.
