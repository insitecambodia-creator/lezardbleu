## 3. Company overview

### Proposed full description

Lézard Bleu is a well-established framing business in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, with a shop on Street 240. Art framing sits at the heart of the business, complemented by large-format printing on quality paper and a retail offering of Khmer antique objects and artifacts.

The business brings together the presentation of images and artworks with an appreciation for objects and visual culture. Customers can approach Lézard Bleu to discuss framing an artwork, printing an image, or exploring the objects available in the shop.

The website should communicate these three activities clearly, with framing as the principal service. Photography of actual work, printing examples and shop objects should provide the main evidence of the business’s capabilities and character.

### Proposed short description

Lézard Bleu specializes in art framing in Phnom Penh, with large-format printing on quality paper and a Street 240 shop offering Khmer antique objects and artifacts.

### Proposed one-line introduction

Art framing, quality printing and Khmer antique objects in Phnom Penh.

## 4. Services and retail offering

### 4.1 Art framing — the core activity

Framing should be the strongest message on the website. Visitors should immediately understand what Lézard Bleu does and how to discuss their artwork with the shop.

**Proposed service copy**

An artwork deserves a presentation that complements its character. Lézard Bleu specializes in art framing, helping customers bring their images and artworks into their homes, workplaces and display spaces. Visit the shop on Street 240 to discuss your piece and the available framing options.

**Content to gather**

- Photographs of completed frames and framed artworks.
- Available frame styles, materials, finishes and sizes.
- Whether made-to-measure framing is available for every advertised category.
- Whether mounts, glazing, canvas stretching and other finishing services are offered.
- Typical process, lead times and quotation requirements.
- Any supported conservation materials or specialist techniques.

Do not advertise museum-grade framing, UV protection, acid-free materials, restoration or conservation expertise unless those services and specifications are confirmed.

### 4.2 Large-format printing

Printing is a distinct service and should have its own explanation and examples. The supplied information confirms a large printer and quality paper, but does not establish the printer model, ink technology, maximum dimensions or paper range.

**Proposed service copy**

Bring your images into print with Lézard Bleu’s large-format printing service. With quality paper and framing available through the same business, customers can discuss both the printed image and its final presentation with the shop.

**Content to gather**

- Printer make and model, maximum print width and supported lengths.
- Paper brands, finishes, weights and available formats.
- Supported file formats, colour profiles and recommended resolution.
- Whether image preparation, scanning, retouching or colour proofing is available.
- Whether customers can send files online and how large files should be transferred.
- Pricing method, turnaround and collection arrangements.

Use “large-format printing on quality paper” until technical specifications are confirmed. Do not introduce claims such as giclée, archival pigment printing, guaranteed colour accuracy or guaranteed print longevity without supporting information.

### 4.3 Khmer antique objects and artifacts

The Street 240 shop also sells Khmer antique objects and artifacts. This offering should have a clear place on the website while allowing the framing service to remain the main focus.

**Proposed service copy**

Visit Lézard Bleu on Street 240 to discover the Khmer antique objects and artifacts available in the shop. Contact the team to ask about a particular item or to find out more about the current selection.

For individual objects, publish only confirmed descriptions of material, dimensions, age, origin, condition and provenance. Distinguish antiques, contemporary crafts and reproductions when applicable. Avoid generic claims of authenticity, archaeological origin, certification, export eligibility or investment value.

If items are shown online, include an availability note and a direct enquiry option. Treat an online shop with checkout, shipping or stock management as a separate feature requiring confirmation.

## 6. Brand voice

The writing should feel knowledgeable, welcoming, clear and visually sensitive. Use concrete descriptions of the work and materials rather than unsupported superlatives.

- Lead with the service or object being discussed.
- Use short paragraphs and accessible English.
- Give visitors a practical next step.
- Support statements with real photographs and confirmed specifications.
- Avoid “best in Cambodia,” “museum quality,” “centuries of heritage” and similar claims unless substantiated.
- Do not invent testimonials, client names, awards or years of experience.

Suggested calls to action: **Discuss your framing project**, **Ask about printing**, **Enquire about an item**, and **Visit the shop**.

“Small H2” describes a visual style. Apply semantic heading levels according to the content hierarchy, even when another element uses that visual treatment.

## 10. Content and photography requirements

Prioritize original, current images: the shop exterior, interior, completed frames, close-ups of finishes, printing equipment, printed results and individual shop objects.

For each image, retain a descriptive filename, an accurate caption where useful, alt text and confirmation that the business can publish it. Do not use a mockup as evidence of a completed project. Identify illustrative imagery when its nature could mislead visitors.

The attached canvas-material research may inform future editorial content about painting supports. It does not establish which canvases Lézard Bleu sells, uses or recommends. Validate any proposed product claims with the business before publication.

## 11. Website implementation and migration brief

Build the presentation pages in Astro, using reusable layouts and a clear separation between content, styles and components. Use Claude Code as the development assistant and keep the source in a Git repository. Prepare deployment configuration for Cloudflare after confirming the hosting approach.

- Keep text and gallery records easy to locate and edit.
- Optimize images and minimize unnecessary browser JavaScript.
- Make navigation, galleries and enquiry actions usable on mobile and with a keyboard.
- Provide unique page titles, descriptions, canonical URLs and social sharing images.
- Generate a sitemap and use accurate structured business data.
- Add a CMS if independent client editing is required; confirm this before finalizing content storage.
- If a contact form is required, define its delivery service, spam protection, success and failure states, and privacy handling.

Before switching the domain, inventory existing pages, metadata, images, forms and Crocoblock features. Preserve suitable URLs and map changed URLs to relevant permanent redirects. Copy required assets so the replacement does not depend on the retired WordPress installation.

Keep the current site available while the replacement is reviewed. Verify contact actions, redirects, image loading, mobile layout and deployment before switching. Preserve existing email DNS records and keep a recoverable backup of the old site.

Handover should include repository and hosting access, build instructions, content-editing guidance, a list of external services and a rollback procedure.
